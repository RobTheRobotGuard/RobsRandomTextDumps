# Too Many Rewards -- Full Reward List

This is the complete list of every reward this addon adds to **Vanilla Storytellers Expanded - Winston Waves**. Every entry below is gated behind its listed mod being active (`MayRequire`) -- see `SupportedMods.md` for the full list of supported mods. None of this content loads, and none of it can error, on a modlist that doesn't have the relevant mod installed.

**289 supported mods, 817 rewards total.**

Reward tiers, from least to most rare: **Poor** -> **Normal** -> **Good** -> **Excellent** -> **Legendary**. Earlier waves mostly roll Poor/Normal rewards; later waves skew toward the higher tiers.

Item and pawn names below are auto-generated from the source mods' internal identifiers, so a few may read slightly technical rather than as clean in-game names.

Some mods on this list are not on the Steam Workshop.
Mainly these non Steam Workshop mods originate from Loverslab.
Although there are only a few mods that are from Loverslab and their account for under 10 entrys on this list.

## [EXS] Rim Exosuit Battalion

*Requires: `nanakud.mvtacticalsurfacefighter`*

**EXOSUIT F4 Phantom Drop** -- *Excellent / Combat*

A cargo pod slams down containing a decommissioned F4 Phantom boarding exosuit frame and one WS-16A 20mm machine gun module -- suit up and become a walking tank.

Grants: 1x EXS TSF F4 Core; 1x EXS TSF Module WS 16 A.

**Defecting Exosuit Pilot** -- *Legendary / Combat*

A battle-hardened exosuit pilot defects to your cause -- and brings their entire war machine with them. Frame, weapon module, and pilot all included.

Grants: 1x random pawn (combat power 150–220, Humanlike); 1x EXS TSF F4 Core; 1x EXS TSF Module WS 16 A.

## [Foxhole x Rimworld] Persistent Warfare

*Requires: `maxaiz.foxhole.persistentwarfare`*

**Colonial Legion Rifleman Kit** -- *Normal / Combat*

A full Colonial Legion rifleman's kit -- Argenti rifle, Legionary Fatigues, and helmet -- arrives crated exactly as issued to Legion regulars, still smelling of gun oil.

Grants: 1x Gun Argenti (Normal quality); 1x Apparel Colonial Uniform (Normal quality); 1x Apparel Colonial Helmet (Normal quality).

**Warden Gunner's Loadout** -- *Good / Combat*

A Warden gunner's full set -- Booker storm rifle, Gunner's Breastplate, and plate helmet -- shows up ready to issue, Warden decals and all.

Grants: 1x Gun Booker (Good quality); 1x Apparel Warden Uniform Armor (Good quality); 1x Apparel Warden Helmet Armor (Good quality).

**Foxhole Heavy Ordnance Crate** -- *Excellent / Combat*

A Gast heavy machine gun and a Neville anti-tank rifle, the two guns every trench line begs command for and never gets enough of, land together in one crate.

Grants: 1x Gun Gast (Excellent quality); 1x Gun Neville (Excellent quality).

**I Saw a Bear** -- *Excellent / Combat*

It's back. The one you swore you saw near the treeline last wave. Turns out it's tame now -- fully grown, faintly smells of gunpowder, and yes, it's still carrying that artillery shell nobody's brave enough to take off it.

Grants: 1x Bear ISAB.
